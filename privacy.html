Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

clip-farm is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and uploads videos to YouTube channels that the owner controls. It has no users other than its owner.

Data accessed

When you sign in with Google, clip-farm asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the YouTube channel you choose (youtube.upload), and
  • read basic statistics, such as view counts, for videos on that channel (youtube.readonly).

How it is used

Data is used only to upload clips and to record how many views each clip gets. clip-farm does not read comments, subscribers, messages or any other account data.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and view counts are stored only on the computer where clip-farm runs. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube API.

Sharing

clip-farm does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Revoking access

You can revoke clip-farm's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files removes all stored credentials.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/KingPudding5/clip-farm-site.
