Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

clip-farm is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and uploads videos to YouTube channels that the owner controls. It has no users other than its owner.

YouTube API Services

clip-farm uses YouTube API Services. By using clip-farm you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Data accessed

When you sign in with Google, clip-farm asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the YouTube channel you choose (youtube.upload), and
  • read basic information about that channel and statistics such as view counts for its videos (youtube.readonly).

How it is used

Data is used only to upload clips and to record how many views each clip gets. clip-farm does not read comments, subscribers, messages or any other account data.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and view counts are stored only on the computer where clip-farm runs. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube API. clip-farm does not use cookies and does not place or read any information on your device's browser.

Sharing

clip-farm does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

  • Statistics read from the YouTube API are refreshed regularly and are not kept for more than 30 days without being refreshed.
  • You can revoke clip-farm's access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • When access is revoked, or on request, all stored tokens and YouTube API data are deleted from the local computer within 7 days.
  • To request deletion, open an issue at the contact link below.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/KingPudding5/clip-farm-site/issues.
